One model in the loop, four jobs.

10 Safety

People stay in charge.

Asks before acting

In the zone, Grok reports and asks: stay and watch, go closer, or fly home. Entering restricted areas or escalating waits for a guard’s yes.

Emergency land

One button, always on screen. It cancels every command and lands every drone.

Lost signal, safe return

If the Wi-Fi drops, the drone backs up along its own path, flies home and lands, or lands where it is after 12 s.

One pilot per drone

Whoever takes off flies it until it lands. Nobody else can command it.

Keeps its distance

80 cm from pillars, 50 cm from walls and tables, and stops 60 cm before anything the camera sees.

Limits on the server

Move sizes, battery and speed limits are enforced in code, not left to the AI.

Set up a site
  1. 1Put each drone on the site Wi-Fi with one script.
  2. 2Add the Grok key and the drones’ addresses.
  3. 3Start everything with one command.
  4. 4Open a link on each phone: it becomes a camera.
  5. 5Draw the red zones on the plan. Done.

Off-the-shelf hardware, nothing to install on the phones, and the site’s own cameras next.
