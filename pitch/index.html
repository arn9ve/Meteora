10 Safety

People stay in charge.

Asks before acting

In the zone, Grok reports and asks: stay and watch, go closer, or fly home.

Emergency land

One button, always on screen. It cancels every command and lands every drone.

Lost signal, safe return

If the Wi-Fi drops, the drone backs up along its path and flies home, or lands after 12 s.

One pilot per drone

Whoever takes off flies it until it lands. Nobody else can command it.

Keeps its distance

80 cm from pillars, 50 cm from walls, and stops 60 cm before anything it sees.

Limits in code

Move sizes, battery and speed limits are enforced by the server, not left to the AI.
